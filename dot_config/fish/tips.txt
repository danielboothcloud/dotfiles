# One tip per line. Shown by fish (config.fish) and the zjstatus bar (zellij/tip).
Zellij: press Ctrl-G to unlock, then E to open Yazi.
Zellij: press Cmd-Shift-F to show or hide floating panes, even while locked.
Yazi: press s to search for files with fd.
Atuin: press ? at the shell prompt to open the AI modal.
Zoxide: run cdi to fuzzy-find a directory.
Fish: run fvi to fuzzy-pick files to open in Neovim.
Zellij: press Ctrl-G, then S to open the session picker.
Atuin: press Tab to edit a selected command; Enter runs it.
Atuin: run atuin resume to pick up an AI agent conversation; --in <agent> continues it in another harness.
Neovim: press Space, then cd to open Yazi at the current file.
Neovim: press Space, then gg to open Lazygit.
Neovim: press Space Space to find files; Space rg searches contents.
Neovim: press Space gf to open the current file’s Git history.
Neovim: press Space mr to toggle rendered Markdown.
Neovim: press Tab / Shift-Tab to cycle buffers.
Zellij: press Ctrl-G, then P, then E to switch the current pane between tiled and floating.
Zellij: press Ctrl-G, then T, then B to move the current pane into its own tab.
Zellij: press Ctrl-G, then T, then S to sync input to all panes in the tab; repeat to turn it off.
Ghostty: Cmd-click a URL to open it from terminal output.
Clones: run clones to browse remote and local repositories.
Fish: px expands to pnpm dlx.
Kubernetes: run kube to toggle the Kubernetes context in your Starship prompt.
Neovim: press Space gb to open Git Browse.
Neovim: ciw replaces the word under your cursor.
Neovim: ci" / ci( replaces everything inside quotes or parentheses.
Neovim: dap deletes the current paragraph.
Neovim: . repeats your last edit.
Neovim: >> / << indents or unindents a line.
Neovim: f<char> jumps to a character on the line; ; repeats, , reverses.
Neovim: % jumps between matching brackets.
Neovim: * searches for the word under your cursor; n finds the next match.
Neovim: Ctrl-O jumps back to your previous location.
Neovim: g; jumps to your previous edit location.
Neovim: gv reselects your last visual selection.
Neovim: Ctrl-A / Ctrl-X increments or decrements a number.
Pi: Ctrl-L cycles through your configured models.
Pi: Ctrl-T shows or hides thinking blocks.
Pi: type @ to find and attach a file.
Pi: !command runs a command and shares its output; !!command keeps it out of the model’s context.
Pi: Option-S opens the subagent interface to observe subagents.
Pi: Ctrl-P lets you copy output from quote and code blocks.
Pi: /review opens tuicr to review agent changes.
Pi: /advisor shows the advisor; /advisor on|off overrides the recipe for this session.
Pi: /handoff <goal> starts a new focused session from an editable summary instead of compaction.
Fish: run ded to fuzzy-find a dotfile, edit it, and apply it with chezmoi.
